The Life of Krishna

            In Mathura's prison, beneath the midnight sky,

            A divine child was born as stars shone high.

            To Devaki and Vasudeva, a blessing from above, 

            Krishna arrived with wisdom, courage, and love.


The Life of Krishna

        Across the Yamuna, through the storm and rain,

        Vasudeva carried him through darkness and pain.

        To Gokul he went, where the little child grew,

        Among loving hears and skies of deepest blue.

With butter in his hands and mischief in his eye He danced through the village beneath sunny skies.
